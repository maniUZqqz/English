Present Simple Tense

Learn how to talk about habits, facts, and regular actions

What is Present Simple?

The Present Simple tense is used to talk about regular actions, facts, habits, and general truths. It's one of the most commonly used tenses in English.

Structure

For positive sentences, we use the base form of the verb for I/you/we/they and add -s or -es for he/she/it.

Examples:
- I work every day.
- She works every day.
- They play tennis on weekends.
- He plays tennis on weekends.

Common Uses

1. Daily routines and habits
2. General truths and facts
3. Permanent situations
4. Timetables and schedules

Examples:
- The sun rises in the east. (fact)
- I usually drink coffee in the morning. (habit)
- The train leaves at 8:00 AM. (schedule)
- She lives in London. (permanent situation)
